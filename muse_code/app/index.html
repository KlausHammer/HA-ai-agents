Before you start

  • The agents act without asking for confirmation (YOLO mode). They run commands, edit files and call Home Assistant on their own.
  • This app has full access: all of Home Assistant's configuration, every app, backups, updates, the network and the host.
  • What you type, and what the agents read here, is sent to the AI vendor of that tab (Meta, Anthropic, Google or OpenAI).
  • Make a full backup before big changes. You can turn YOLO off per agent in Settings (gear icon) or in the app options.
